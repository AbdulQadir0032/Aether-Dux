About Aether Dux

Engineering a more capable sky.

Aether Dux is an aerial technology company focused on making autonomous flight useful, dependable and mission-ready across Nigeria and beyond.

Our vision

A future where distance is no longer a barrier.

We envision a world where intelligent aerial systems extend the reach of people, institutions and essential services without being limited by conventional infrastructure.

Our work sits at the intersection of robotics, artificial intelligence, aerospace engineering and real-world logistics.
